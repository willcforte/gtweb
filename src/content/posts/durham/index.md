# Durham Boxes

## Overview

A durable storage method for screws and other small components; works great with Gridfinity-style bins (see [Caden Kraft's custom-fit bins for Durham 123-95/107-04](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1390642-durham-parts-storage-bins-with-gridfinity-labels#profileId-1440768)).
