# Compendium on Robotic Locomotion

Locomotion is a marvel of evolution that I have been fascinated with for many years. Not only does its study heighten one's appreciation for nature's engineering acumen, but, like any good problem, it makes demands from many fields, giving you an excuse to learn a lot in the process!

Good readings on this subject can be found in the syallabus of MIT 2.74 Biomimetic Robotics by Sangbae Kim.

[Alexander1984](https://www.jstor.org/stable/pdf/27852758.pdf) is a good introduction to basic walking mechanics.
